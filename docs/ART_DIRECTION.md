# PANG DAILY — ART DIRECTION

## 1. Visual Identity

Pang Daily uses a **lo-fi nocturnal arcade realism** style.

The target feel is:

> **PS2-era dream racer meets modern Iloilo car culture.**

The game should combine:

- early-2000s arcade racing nostalgia
- grainy late-night car photography
- stylized low-fidelity realism
- warm Filipino roadside environments
- moody tropical night lighting
- recognizable Iloilo-inspired spaces

The game should not chase photorealism.

Mood, familiarity, and car presence matter more than technical fidelity.

---

## 2. Visual Pillars

### 2.1 Cars Are the Heroes

Cars should receive the most visual attention.

They should have:

- clear silhouettes
- strong stance
- readable wheel fitment
- convincing reflections
- expressive headlights and taillights
- visible ride height changes
- strong paint response under streetlights
- simple but attractive materials

Cars do not need extremely high polygon counts.

They need to look good in motion and under dramatic lighting.

---

### 2.2 Familiarity Over Spectacle

Environment design should prioritize recognition.

The player should see:

- coffee shops
- gas stations
- convenience stores
- talyers
- vulcanizing shops
- car washes
- roadside houses
- concrete walls
- utility poles
- tangled wires
- drainage
- roadside vegetation
- parked motorcycles
- tricycles
- small eateries
- provincial roads

The goal is not "wow, huge city."

The goal is:

> "This feels like somewhere I've actually been."

---

### 2.3 Night Is the Signature Mood

The game should support daytime, but its strongest identity is:

- dusk
- night
- late night
- post-rain evening
- early morning before sunrise

Night allows:

- stronger car lighting
- better atmosphere
- more dramatic reflections
- cleaner visual focus
- better use of lo-fi post-processing

---

### 2.4 Imperfection Is Good

The world should not feel sterile.

Use:

- worn concrete
- faded paint
- rust
- imperfect roads
- patched pavement
- old signs
- mismatched building materials
- cheap fluorescent lights
- messy shops
- cluttered talyers
- dirty parking lots

The game should feel lived in.

---

## 3. Rendering Style

Target:

**PS2 / early PS3 fidelity with modern lighting and post-processing**

Recommended characteristics:

- moderate-poly vehicles
- low/moderate-poly environments
- baked or simplified lighting where possible
- selective dynamic lighting
- controlled reflections
- volumetric-style fog where affordable
- soft image output
- intentional grain

Avoid:

- ultra-sharp modern realism
- overly clean PBR showroom surfaces
- giant texture budgets
- excessive geometry
- overcomplicated shaders

---

## 4. Post-Processing

Post-processing is part of the art style.

Recommended effects:

- film grain
- subtle bloom
- mild chromatic aberration
- slight lens distortion
- soft motion blur
- controlled vignette
- slight temporal softness
- optional VHS / digicam filters for menus or photo mode

Use restraint.

The image should remain readable.

---

## 5. Color Direction

### Base World Palette

- charcoal
- asphalt gray
- concrete beige
- faded green
- dark tropical foliage
- muted building paint
- rust brown
- warm gray

### Lighting Palette

- sodium orange
- fluorescent white-green
- warm café yellow
- cool moonlight
- red taillights
- yellow-white headlights
- occasional neon or aftermarket color accents

### Rule

The world stays relatively muted.

Cars, gauges, lights, and signage provide stronger color accents.

---

## 6. Car Art Direction

Cars should be fictionalized but clearly inspired by common local 1990s–2000s sedans and compacts.

Starter inspirations:

- Lancer-like sedan
- Civic-like sedan/hatch
- Corolla-like sedan
- Sentra-like sedan

Car models should support:

- wheel swaps
- different wheel offsets
- ride height
- exterior body parts
- spoilers
- bumpers
- hood variants
- simple paint customization

Initial customization priority:

1. wheels
2. ride height
3. exterior body parts
4. paint
5. lights

Interior customization can come later.

---

## 7. Environment Art Direction

### Coffee Shop Hub

Inspired by a real local tambay coffee shop.

Should contain:

- simple modern building
- outdoor chairs
- parking directly in front
- warm interior lighting
- a social cluster area
- local road context
- recognizable evening mood

The place should look good with several parked cars.

---

### Gas Station

Should function as a bright nighttime social space.

Characteristics:

- strong overhead canopy lighting
- bright reflections on cars
- convenience-store adjacency
- clean central area
- darker surrounding road
- natural meeting point

Do not copy exact Shell branding unless legally cleared.

Use a fictional equivalent.

---

### Convenience Store

Inspired by familiar 24-hour convenience stores.

Functions visually as:

- bright landmark
- parking stop
- pre-run meetup
- contrast against dark roads

Use fictional branding.

---

### Talyer

Should feel:

- fluorescent
- cluttered
- oily
- practical
- slightly cramped
- familiar

Props:

- plastic chairs
- tools
- old calendars
- loose wheels
- engine parts
- electric fan
- welding gear
- oil containers
- spare bumpers
- random motorcycle
- dog sleeping nearby

---

### Mountain Road

Should preserve the rhythm of real Iloilo mountain roads.

Art features:

- tropical vegetation
- narrow road feel
- concrete sections
- patched asphalt
- roadside homes
- occasional stores
- guardrails
- utility poles
- fog
- rain
- low lighting
- viewpoints
- small landmarks

The road should feel like a real place, not a closed race circuit.

---

## 8. Traffic Art Direction

Traffic should look ordinary.

Include fictionalized equivalents of:

- old sedans
- small hatchbacks
- vans
- tricycles
- motorcycles
- jeepney-like public transport
- delivery trucks

Traffic vehicles can use lower detail than player cars.

---

## 9. Character Art Direction

Characters should use stylized realism.

Avoid expensive high-fidelity humans.

Target:

- recognizable silhouettes
- expressive clothing
- simple faces
- believable proportions
- readable animations

Character identity should come from:

- posture
- outfit
- hairstyle
- accessories
- dialogue
- car association

Cars and locations should receive more visual polish than characters.

---

## 10. UI Art Direction

Two distinct UI modes should exist.

### Driving HUD

More arcade-like.

Can include:

- analog-style tachometer
- speedometer
- gear
- timer
- race position
- opponent gap
- warning lights
- navigation arrows

Visual inspiration:

- early-2000s tuner games
- aftermarket gauges
- arcade racing cabinets
- dashboard lighting

---

### Life / Menu UI

More grounded and personal.

Includes:

- phone
- marketplace
- messages
- garage
- finances
- jobs
- map
- relationships

Visual direction:

- practical
- slightly rough
- familiar
- local
- modern but not futuristic

Marketplace screens can evoke local buy/sell culture without directly cloning Facebook.

---

## 11. Photo / Memory Style

The game should use a grainy digicam / late-night tuner-photo style for:

- loading screens
- chapter cards
- race intros
- post-race shots
- garage photos
- social media posts
- Marketplace listings

This can include:

- flash photography
- grain
- compression
- slight blur
- timestamp-like overlays
- dark backgrounds
- colored car lighting

---

## 12. Weather

Key weather states:

- humid clear night
- light rain
- heavy rain
- foggy mountain
- cloudy afternoon
- post-rain wet roads

Wet roads should be visually important.

They should create:

- reflections
- glare
- atmospheric lighting
- stronger mood

---

## 13. Lighting Rules

### Car Rule

Cars should always receive readable rim or reflection lighting when possible.

### Night Rule

The world can be dark, but important roads and objects must remain readable.

### Shop Rule

Talyers, convenience stores, cafés, and gas stations should create distinct lighting islands.

### Mountain Rule

Darkness should feel atmospheric, not unfair.

Headlights must matter.

---

## 14. Asset Production Targets

Recommended vehicle budget:

- roughly 8k–30k triangles per main car depending on importance
- lower detail LODs for traffic and distant vehicles

Recommended environment budget:

- modular
- instanced
- repeated where appropriate
- optimized for browser rendering

Texture targets:

- 512–2048px depending on asset importance
- larger textures only for hero assets
- use atlases where practical

Primary format:

- glTF / GLB

---

## 15. AI-Assisted Asset Rules

AI-generated models, textures, UI concepts, sounds, and reference art may be used.

All generated assets must be checked for:

- consistency
- topology
- scale
- UV quality
- material count
- optimization
- legal safety
- visual fit

AI output is a starting point, not final production quality.

---

## 16. Art Guardrails

### Do

- prioritize mood
- use strong nighttime lighting
- make old cars look heroic
- embrace grain and imperfection
- make local details specific
- use familiar roadside clutter
- preserve readable silhouettes
- let ugly builds still look lovable

### Do Not

- go cyberpunk
- make everything neon
- over-modernize Iloilo
- make every road look Japanese
- chase AAA realism
- make the world sterile
- make characters more detailed than cars
- overuse blur or grain until the game becomes unreadable

---

## 17. Art Direction Summary

Pang Daily should look like:

> **A grainy, dreamy, low-budget late-night racing memory set in Iloilo — where fluorescent talyers, coffee shop parking lots, old sedans, mountain roads, gas stations, and wet pavement make ordinary cars feel legendary.**
